# Weekly research - 2026-11-17 (vidIQ)

Credits used: 15 (balance was 150/month, resets on the 2nd).

## What is breaking out around Napoleon (last ~3 months)
- YouTube Shorts: dominated by music "edits" (15-45 s, no narration, slowed/phonk tracks such as "Ainsi Bas La Vida"): 5.9M, 533K, 559K views on channels under 7K subs. Pure aura, no story.
- TikTok/Instagram narrated stories that broke out (closest to our format):
  - "1vs6?" opening on a map of Europe with flags (17.7M, 2.5M, 597K, 1M views). Map + one provocative on-screen question in the first frame.
  - "Don't bully in school in case you bully Napoleon..." (891K): paintings + voiceover, ironic hook about his childhood.
  - How Napoleon was chosen to command the Army of Italy (2.5M, 30 s): map + portraits + voiceover.
  - Waterloo explained as "Part 1" of a series (157K) and "Who is responsible for the loss at Waterloo?" (4.2M).
- Rising searches (month): "what if history" +175%, "war stories" +118%.

## Lessons applied
1. First frame = a strong on-screen line or a map, never a slow establishing shot. Maps of Europe with flags perform best as openers.
2. A provocative question as on-screen hook works ("1 vs 6?", "Who lost Waterloo?").
3. Childhood/underdog angle (bullied Corsican boy) performs: use it as a flash in series, and as its own series.
4. Waterloo is the most searched and most viral battle: move it up.
5. "What if" history is rising: a "What if Napoleon had won Waterloo?" series (clearly labelled as speculation) after the real Waterloo series.
