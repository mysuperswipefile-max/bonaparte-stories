In November 1796, the most promising young general in Europe was three days away from losing everything.
He was twenty-seven. His army was exhausted, outnumbered, and caught between two enemy armies.
To escape, he gambled everything on one narrow bridge, in the middle of a swamp.
His friend would die at his side. He himself would end up face down in a muddy ditch.
And yet, the painting of that day would turn him into a legend.
This is the full story of the battle of Arcole. The battle where Napoleon almost lost.
