## Rules from what performs
- Open part 1 of every series on a map (Europe or the theatre of war) with flags or arrows; open other parts on the most dramatic image available.
- The hook line must work as on-screen text on its own: short, provocative, ideally a question or a paradox ("1 against 6?", "Who really lost Waterloo?").
- Underdog and childhood angles (the mocked Corsican boy) hook well: keep a short flash of them when relevant.
- Prefer battles and moments people already search for (Waterloo, Austerlitz, Russia 1812) to grow reach; alternate with lesser-known gems.
- Keep every part at 61+ seconds (monetisation), but make the first 3 seconds as dense as a 30-second viral video.
